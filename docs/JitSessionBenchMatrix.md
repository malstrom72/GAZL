> directory. This is the record the two plan documents beside it refer to for what shipped.

Milestones: **pre** = 1eee9b2 session baseline; **pool** = 6f0ad7a RIP float pool + 16B align (x64); **div** = ea068ba div/mod cold traps; **fix** = b09affe v2.3a-lite soundness fix; **realm** = 0cfe34a v2.3a-full realm stamping; **maps1** = 1f1b689 v2.2 shared maps (gated); **maps2** = 114fb44 v2.2 varying maps (HEAD)

Method: single process per cell, --bench=12 --warmup=4 (firmwares --bench=8 --warmup=3 via runPermut8Firmware).
Small-firmware cells carry code-layout noise (a few percent band); trust the geomean trend + the large kernels.

the v2.3a-lite unsoundness skipped required flushes until the `fix` milestone; their apparent regression at `fix`
is the price of correctness.
